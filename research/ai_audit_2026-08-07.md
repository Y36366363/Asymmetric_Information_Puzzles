# AI mechanism audit — 2026-08-07

This is a backend-only experiment. It calls the Python Battleship simulator and
does not open or alter the web game. The run is reproducible with:

```bash
PYTHONPATH=src python research/audit_battleship_ai.py --games 100 --seed 20260807
```

## Cross-scale results

| Board | Policy | Mean shots | Median | P90 | Best–worst |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 10×10 | Random | 95.48 | 97 | 100 | 81–100 |
| 10×10 | Hunt/target | 56.85 | 59 | 67 | 32–70 |
| 10×10 | Probability density | 45.62 | 45.5 | 58 | 28–68 |
| 12×12 | Random | 138.94 | 141 | 144 | 110–144 |
| 12×12 | Hunt/target | 77.88 | 79.5 | 93 | 43–103 |
| 12×12 | Probability density | 64.12 | 63.5 | 81 | 39–95 |
| 15×15 | Random | 218.10 | 221 | 225 | 191–225 |
| 15×15 | Hunt/target | 117.77 | 118.5 | 142 | 73–148 |
| 15×15 | Probability density | 95.86 | 94 | 119 | 63–147 |

Probability density reduced mean shots versus hunt/target by 19.8% on 10×10,
17.7% on 12×12, and 18.6% on 15×15. Its advantage therefore persists as the
action space grows rather than coming only from the standard board.

## Tail-risk check

On 100 identical 10×10 hidden fleets, probability density beat hunt/target 76
times, tied once, and lost 23 times. It saved 9.1 shots on average, while both
the largest gain and largest regret were 34 shots.

The important conclusion is not that the current AI is optimal. It is a strong,
auditable one-step heuristic whose average advantage is stable, but random tie
breaking and local density choices can still produce a poor individual game.
Future evolution should compare a cluster-aware posterior or limited lookahead
against this paired baseline, and accept it only if both mean shots and P90 tail
improve without making interactive decisions noticeably slower.

## Reliability finding

The Bulls and Cows adviser retained every previously evaluated information set
for the lifetime of a local session. Repeated research games could therefore
grow memory without a fixed ceiling. The cache now uses least-recently-used
eviction and retains at most 256 information states; the shared immutable 5,040
code worlds remain cached separately.
